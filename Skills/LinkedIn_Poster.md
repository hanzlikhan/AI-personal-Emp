# @LinkedIn_Poster

## Description
Generates and posts business content to LinkedIn to generate sales and engagement. Creates professional, value-driven content that aligns with company messaging.

## Purpose
- Generate engaging LinkedIn content that drives business value
- Post content at optimal times for maximum reach
- Track engagement metrics and performance
- Ensure all content follows brand guidelines

## Triggers
- `@LinkedIn_Poster` - Initiate content generation and posting
- `post to LinkedIn` - Post specific content to LinkedIn
- `linkedin content` - Generate LinkedIn-specific content
- `generate post` - Create new LinkedIn post

## Steps
1. Analyze current trending topics in industry
2. Generate content ideas based on business objectives
3. Draft LinkedIn post following these guidelines:
   - Professional tone
   - Value-driven content
   - Clear call-to-action
   - Relevant hashtags
4. Submit draft to /Pending_Approval/ for human review
5. Wait for approval before posting
6. Once approved, schedule/post to LinkedIn
7. Track engagement metrics (likes, shares, comments)
8. Report performance in dashboard

## @-mention Usage
- Use `@LinkedIn_Poster` to generate and post content
- Include topic or theme when requesting specific content
- Combine with `@Approval_Handler` for approval workflow

## Handbook Reference
See Company_Handbook.md section 4.1 for LinkedIn posting guidelines and approval requirements.

## Ralph Wiggum Loop Prevention
- All content must be approved before posting
- Never post without verifying it adds business value
- Follow brand voice and messaging guidelines strictly
- Maintain content calendar to prevent overposting
